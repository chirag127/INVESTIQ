## 1. The Geometry of Negative Compounding

Debt is compounding operating in reverse against your net worth. In India, retail borrowers frequently accumulate multiple fragmented liabilities across credit cards, Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) schemes, personal loans, and consumer durable EMIs without understanding the effective annual percentage rate (APR).

### The True Cost of Indian Borrowing
- **Credit Card Revolving Balance:** 42.0% – 48.0% APR (3.5% – 4.0% monthly interest plus 18% GST on interest charges). Paying only the "Minimum Amount Due" (MAD) is an actuarial trap where an initial ₹50,000 balance takes over 14 years to extinguish while generating ₹1,60,000+ in pure interest payments.
- **BNPL & Instant App Loans:** 28.0% – 36.0% APR hidden under upfront "processing fees" and daily interest penalties.
- **Unsecured Personal Loans:** 11.5% – 18.0% APR.
- **Secured Home Loans:** 8.25% – 9.25% APR (Repo-linked external benchmark lending rate).

## 2. Avalanche vs Snowball: The Elimination Framework

When tackling multiple debts, you must implement one of two disciplined algorithms:

1. **The Debt Avalanche (Mathematically Optimal):**
   - Rank debts strictly by interest rate in descending order (highest APR first).
   - Pay the statutory minimum due on all debts.
   - Channel 100% of remaining monthly debt-service surplus into the highest-rate debt until extinguished, then cascade into the next.
   - *Result:* Minimizes total lifetime rupees lost to interest.

2. **The Debt Snowball (Psychologically Optimal):**
   - Rank debts by outstanding principal balance in ascending order (smallest balance first).
   - Direct all surplus to eliminating the smallest balance regardless of interest rate.
   - *Result:* Provides quick psychological victories and eliminates debt accounts rapidly.

## 3. Statutory Legal Rights Against Recovery Agents

Under the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Fair Practices Code** and circulars on Loan Recovery by NBFCs and Banks:
1. **Calling Hours:** Recovery agents are legally prohibited from contacting borrowers before 8:00 AM or after 7:00 PM.
2. **Harassment & Intimidation:** Any verbal abuse, threats of physical harm, public humiliation, or contacting friends/family/colleagues not listed as legal co-borrowers or guarantors is strictly illegal under RBI Master Directions.
3. **Data Privacy:** Lending apps are barred from accessing mobile contacts, location, or gallery photos.
4. **Grievance Route:** In the event of harassment, lodge a complaint with the bank's Principal Nodal Officer. If unresolved within 30 days, file directly on the **RBI Complaint Management System (CMS)** portal under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

## 4. Arjun's Case File: The Debt Rescue

::: casefile-arjun
### Arjun's Liabilities
- Credit Card Balance: ₹35,000 at 44% APR.
- Laptop Consumer EMI: ₹20,000 remaining at 16% APR.
- Educational Loan: ₹1,20,000 at 9.5% APR.

### Execution
Arjun ceases all non-essential discretionary spending for 3 months. Applying the Avalanche protocol:
1. Month 1–2: Wipes out the ₹35,000 credit card balance completely, immediately halting a ₹1,300/month interest bleed.
2. Month 3: Extinguishes the remaining laptop EMI.
3. Educational Loan: Transitions to regular monthly payments, as the 9.5% interest rate is sustainable and qualifies for tax deduction under the Old Regime if chosen.
:::
