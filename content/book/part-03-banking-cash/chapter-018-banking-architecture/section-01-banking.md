## 1. The Indian Banking Hierarchy

Understanding the statutory backing of your liquid deposits protects you from institutional bank runs. The Reserve Bank of India classifies banking institutions into four distinct regulatory tiers:

1. **Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs):** State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank. Designated by the RBI as "Too Big to Fail," subjecting them to heightened capital adequacy and regulatory scrutiny.
2. **Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs):** Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, etc.
3. **Small Finance Banks (SFBs):** AU Small Finance Bank, Equitas, Ujjivan, etc. Subject to standard RBI prudential norms and 75% priority sector lending mandates.
4. **Co-operative Banks:** Urban and rural co-operative credit societies. Historically plagued by dual-regulation loopholes (State Registrar of Co-operative Societies + RBI) and significantly elevated insolvency risk (e.g. PMC Bank, Punjab & Maharashtra Co-operative).

## 2. Deposit Insurance (DICGC) Mechanics

Under the **Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) Act, 1961** (amended 2021):
- **Coverage Limit:** Deposits up to **₹5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees)** per depositor per insured bank are guaranteed by sovereign-backed deposit insurance.
- **Aggregation Rule:** All deposits held across branches of the same bank (Savings, Current, Fixed Deposits, Recurring Deposits) are aggregated. If you hold ₹20 Lakhs across 4 fixed deposits in one bank, only ₹5 Lakhs total is insured.
- **Account Capacity Separation:** Deposits held in different legal capacities (e.g., Individual account vs Sole Proprietor vs HUF vs Joint account with different order of names) each enjoy an independent ₹5 Lakh cover.
- **Resolution Timeline:** Under Section 18A of the amended DICGC Act, insured depositors must receive interim payouts within **90 days** if a bank is placed under moratorium or directions by the RBI.

## 3. High-Yield Sweep Accounts (Auto-Sweep FDs)

A standard savings account earning 2.5%–3.0% interest is an inflation casualty. By activating an **Auto-Sweep facility (Reverse Sweep)**:
- Idle balances above a predetermined threshold (e.g., ₹25,000) automatically sweep into multi-tenure Fixed Deposits earning 6.5%–7.5%.
- When cash is withdrawn or cheques clear, funds break in exact units without breaking the entire FD or incurring premature withdrawal penalties.
- *Tax Note:* Sweep interest is treated as fixed deposit interest, taxable at your marginal slab rate under "Income from Other Sources."
