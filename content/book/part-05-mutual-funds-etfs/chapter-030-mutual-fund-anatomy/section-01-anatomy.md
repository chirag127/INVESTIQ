## 1. Direct Plans vs Regular Plans: The Unseen Commission Drag

Under SEBI regulations, every mutual fund scheme in India is mandated to offer two variants:
1. **Direct Plan:** Invested directly through the AMC, MF Central, MFCentral, or direct execution platforms. No distributor commission is paid.
2. **Regular Plan:** Purchased through a broker, distributor, or bank relationship manager. A recurring trail commission of **0.50% to 1.50% annually** is deducted from your daily NAV and paid to the distributor for the entire duration of your investment.

### The 30-Year Compounding Destruction Math
Suppose you invest ₹25,000 per month for 25 years in an equity fund generating a gross 12.0% CAGR:
- **Direct Plan (TER 0.55%):** Net return = 11.45%. Future Corpus = **₹3.92 Crores**.
- **Regular Plan (TER 1.55%):** Net return = 10.45%. Future Corpus = **₹3.18 Crores**.

**The Cost:** You lose **₹74,00,000 (Seventy-Four Lakh Rupees)** in commissions and lost compounding to your distributor, simply for having chosen the "Regular" checkbox.

## 2. SEBI Categorization and The Riskometer

SEBI's 2017 Categorization Circular strictly defines scheme mandates to prevent style drift:
- **Large Cap:** Top 100 stocks by market capitalization (minimum 80% allocation).
- **Mid Cap:** 101st to 250th stock by market capitalization (minimum 65% allocation).
- **Small Cap:** 251st stock onward (minimum 65% allocation).
- **Flexi Cap:** Multi-cap unconstrained with minimum 65% in Indian equities.

Always inspect the Scheme Information Document (SID) and current portfolio factsheet before committing long-term capital.
