## 1. Pure Risk Protection vs Endowment Marketing

In India, life insurance is heavily mis-sold as an investment product through traditional Endowment, Money-Back, and Unit Linked Insurance Plans (ULIPs). 

- **The Problem:** Traditional endowment policies yield a pathetic 4.5%–5.5% IRR while providing an abysmal life cover (typically 10x annual premium, e.g., ₹50,000 premium yields only ₹5,00,000 cover). If an earning member passes away, ₹5 Lakhs does not support an urban family for more than 12 months.
- **The Solution: Pure Term Life Insurance.** You pay a small annual premium solely for mortality risk protection. If you survive the term, the insurer pays nothing; if you die, your nominees receive a substantial, tax-free sum assured (e.g. ₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crores) under Section 10(10D).

## 2. Calculating Your Human Life Value (HLV) Cover

To determine how much life cover you require, apply the **DIME Formula**:

$$\text{Required Cover} = \text{Debt} + \text{Income Replacement} + \text{Mortgage} + \text{Education/Future Goals} - \text{Existing Liquid Assets}$$

### Concrete Rule of Thumb for Tech Professionals
- Maintain a minimum pure term cover of **15x to 20x of your gross annual income** until your retirement corpus reaches full financial independence.
- If your annual salary is ₹15,00,000, your baseline term cover should be **₹2.25 Crores to ₹3.0 Crores**.

## 3. IRDAI Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR) vs Claim Amount Settled

When selecting an insurer regulated by the **Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)**:
- Do not inspect Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR) by number of policies alone (many small rural policies can skew the percentage).
- Inspect **Claim Settlement Ratio by Benefit Amount**: This reveals the percentage of total rupee claims honored versus rejected. Look for insurers consistently clearing 98%+ on both counts.

## 4. Section 45 of the Insurance Act: The 3-Year Incontestability Shield

Under **Section 45 of the Insurance Act, 1938**:
- An insurer can investigate and challenge a policy on grounds of misstatement, fraud, or non-disclosure of medical facts only within the first **3 years** of policy issuance.
- **After 3 years have elapsed**, no life insurance policy can be called into question or repudiated by the insurer on any ground whatsoever, including fraud or non-disclosure.
- *Imperative Action:* Practice **100% Uberrima Fides (Utmost Good Faith)** on your medical proposal form. Declare all pre-existing conditions, smoking/alcohol habits, and family medical history truthfully. Once 3 years pass, your family's financial claim is legally invulnerable.
