## 1. The Unified Capital Gains Framework (FY 2026-27)

Following structural reforms in the Finance Act, the capital gains taxation regime in India has been streamlined into simplified holding period thresholds and statutory rate tiers:

| Asset Category | Short-Term Holding Period | STCG Tax Rate | Long-Term Holding Period | LTCG Tax Rate | Indexation Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listed Equity Shares & Equity Mutual Funds** | $\le 12$ Months | **20.0%** (Sec 111A) | $> 12$ Months | **12.5%** (Sec 112A) | Nil (₹1.25L annual exemption) |
| **Specified Debt Mutual Funds** | Regardless of period | Marginal Slab Rate | Regardless of period | Marginal Slab Rate | Nil |
| **Physical Real Estate / Land** | $\le 24$ Months | Marginal Slab Rate | $> 24$ Months | **12.5%** (Sec 112) | Nil (with grandfathering options) |
| **Physical Gold / Silver & Gold ETFs** | $\le 12$ Months (ETFs) / 24 Mo | Marginal Slab Rate | $> 12$ Mo (ETFs) / 24 Mo | **12.5%** | Nil |
| **Unlisted Shares & Startup Equity** | $\le 24$ Months | Marginal Slab Rate | $> 24$ Months | **12.5%** | Nil |

*(Health and Education Cess of 4% applies on computed tax).*

## 2. The ₹1,25,000 Equity LTCG Exemption Threshold

Under Section 112A:
- Aggregate Long-Term Capital Gains realized from listed Indian equities and equity-oriented mutual funds up to **₹1,25,000 per financial year** are taxed at **0%**.
- Any net LTCG exceeding ₹1,25,000 is taxed at a flat **12.5% plus cess (13.0% effective)**.
- *Legal Tax-Harvesting Practice (L1):* Systematically selling profitable mutual fund units each financial year to realize up to ₹1.25 Lakhs in gains, and immediately repurchasing units to step up your cost of acquisition. This is fully lawful and resets your tax base without tax friction.

## 3. Statutory Loss Set-Off and Carry-Forward Rules

Under Sections 70 and 71 of the Income-tax Act:
1. **Short-Term Capital Loss (STCL):** Can be set off against **both** Short-Term Capital Gains and Long-Term Capital Gains realized during the same financial year.
2. **Long-Term Capital Loss (LTCL):** Can **only** be set off against Long-Term Capital Gains. It cannot be set off against Short-Term Capital Gains, salary, business income, or house property.
3. **Carry Forward:** Unabsorbed capital losses can be carried forward for **8 consecutive assessment years**, provided your Income Tax Return (ITR) is filed on or before the statutory due date under Section 139(1).
