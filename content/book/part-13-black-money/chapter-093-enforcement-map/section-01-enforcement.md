## 1. The Death of Financial Secrecy in India

In contemporary India, the notion that cash transactions or unlinked accounts can remain hidden from state authorities is an obsolete myth. The Government of India and regulatory authorities operate an integrated, real-time data-matching grid that automatically reconciles citizen financial transactions.

---

## 2. The Core Investigation & Enforcement Agencies

1. **Income Tax Department (ITD) & Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT):** Operates the Insight Portal, Project Insight, and the automated verification system. Governs the Income-tax Act, the Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) Act, 2015, and the Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act, 1988.
2. **Enforcement Directorate (ED):** Nodal agency enforcing the **Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA)** and the **Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA)**. Holds extensive powers of arrest, attachment of assets, and international mutual legal assistance.
3. **Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND):** Central national agency responsible for receiving, processing, analyzing, and disseminating information relating to suspect financial transactions. All banks, financial institutions, intermediaries, and crypto exchanges are statutory reporting entities under PMLA.
4. **Directorate General of GST Intelligence (DGGI):** Analyzes fake invoicing, circular trading, and fraudulent Input Tax Credit (ITC) networks using e-way bill analytics and GSTN reconciliation.
5. **Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI):** Monitors market surveillance, insider trading, front-running, and unauthorized collective investment schemes.

## 3. How Financial Data Flows: The Digital Surveillance Architecture

Your financial footprint is continuously reported through four primary mechanisms:
- **Statement of Financial Transactions (SFT):** Mandated under Section 285BA. Banks, mutual funds, registrar offices, and credit card companies must report:
  - Cash deposits or withdrawals aggregating ₹10 Lakhs or more in savings accounts (₹50 Lakhs in current accounts).
  - Credit card payments exceeding ₹1 Lakh in cash or ₹10 Lakhs by any mode annually.
  - Purchase of mutual funds, shares, bonds, or debentures aggregating ₹10 Lakhs or more.
  - Purchase or sale of immovable property valued at ₹30 Lakhs or more (or stamp duty value).
- **Annual Information Statement (AIS) & Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS):** Comprehensive ledger visible on your Income Tax portal displaying all dividends, share sales, interest, foreign remittances, and property deals.
- **Common Reporting Standard (CRS) & FATCA:** Automatic exchange of foreign financial account information between India and over 100 global tax jurisdictions. Unreported foreign bank accounts, shares, or trusts trigger mandatory 120% penalties and up to 10 years rigorous imprisonment under the Black Money Act.
