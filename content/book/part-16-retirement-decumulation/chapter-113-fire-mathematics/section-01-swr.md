## 1. Why the Western "4% Rule" Fails in India

The famous Trinity Study in the United States popularized the "4% Safe Withdrawal Rate" (SWR) for a 30-year retirement horizon. In India, blindly applying a 4% rule is an actuarial disaster because:
1. **Higher Indian Inflation:** US long-term inflation averages 2% to 3%. Indian urban inflation compounds at 6% to 8%.
2. **Longer Retirement Horizons for Early FIRE:** Retiring at age 35 or 40 requires a **45-to-50-year longevity horizon**, not 30 years. Over 50 years, the probability of failure under a 4% initial withdrawal rate with 7% annual inflation exceeds 60%.
3. **Sequence of Returns Risk (SRR):** Experiencing a deep market crash in the first 3 to 5 years of decumulation forces you to sell equity units at depressed prices, permanently impairing the portfolio's compounding capacity.

---

## 2. Indian Safe Withdrawal Rate Recommendation

For an early-retirement or FIRE plan in India:
- **Baseline Safe Withdrawal Rate:** Target **2.75% to 3.25%** initial withdrawal rate.
- **Corpus Sizing:** Multiply your annual non-discretionary expenses by **33x to 36x** (rather than 25x).
- **The Three-Bucket Decumulation Architecture:**
  - **Bucket 1 (Cash & Liquid, Years 1–3):** 3 years of living expenses in arbitrage funds, liquid funds, and high-interest sweep accounts. Immune to equity crashes.
  - **Bucket 2 (Income & Debt, Years 4–8):** 5 years of expenses in target maturity bond funds, corporate debt, and G-Secs.
  - **Bucket 3 (Growth Equity, Years 9+):** Diversified domestic and international equity index funds compounding long-term. Replenishes Bucket 1 during bull markets.

## 3. Arjun's 30-Year FIRE Blueprint

::: casefile-arjun
### The Target
Arjun projects comfortable living expenses of ₹75,000 per month (₹9,00,000 annually) at age 40 in present-day rupees.
### The Math
- Corpus required at 33x annual expenses = ₹9,00,000 × 33 = **₹2,97,00,000 (~₹3 Crores)** in present purchasing power terms.
- Assuming 15 years to age 40 and 6% inflation, the future nominal corpus required is approximately **₹7.2 Crores**.
- With a disciplined monthly SIP of ₹45,000 stepping up by 10% annually with his software salary increments, Arjun reaches this threshold without relying on speculation or family inheritance.
:::
